Busy Board — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

We collect no data. None.

The short version

Busy Board: Toddler Games 2+ is a set of toy walls for young children that runs entirely on your iPad. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it never connects to the internet. We never see who you or your child are or how your child plays.

What stays on your device

What your child makes in the app (for example, the hero built in the workshop), the parent settings and the state of the play timer are stored locally on your iPad. This data never leaves the device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Camera, microphone, photos and location

The app does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, location or contacts and never asks for these permissions. A toy that looks like a camera or a microphone is only a drawing: it works inside the app and does not use the iPad's own camera or microphone.

Advertising, links and purchases

The app shows no advertising. Links that lead out of the app sit in the parents' area behind a parental gate, and so would any purchases. If the app offers purchases, they are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your name, payment details or any billing information. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Network use

The app works fully offline. All graphics and sounds ship inside the app.

Deleting your data

Delete the app — everything it stored on your iPad is removed with it. There is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

Busy Board is made for young children and is listed in the Kids category of the App Store. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information of children (or anyone else) is ever collected, used or shared.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
