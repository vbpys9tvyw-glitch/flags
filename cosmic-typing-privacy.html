Cosmic Typing — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

We collect no data. None.

The short version

Cosmic Typing is a touch-typing game that runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We never see who you are, what you type or how you play.

What stays on your device

Pilot profiles and their names, campaign progress, stars, experience, streaks, per-key typing statistics and settings are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Keyboard input

The app reads which keys you press only while it is on screen, only to play the game. Keystrokes are not recorded as text, stored or sent anywhere — only aggregate counts such as accuracy per key are kept on your device.

Purchases

If the app offers purchases, they are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your name, payment details or any billing information. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Network use

The app works fully offline. All graphics, sounds and music ship inside the app.

Deleting your data

Delete a pilot in the Pilots screen, use “Reset progress” in Settings, or simply delete the app — all locally stored data is removed with it.

Children

Because we collect no data at all, no personal information of children (or anyone else) is ever collected or processed.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
