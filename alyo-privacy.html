Alyo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

We collect no data. None.

The short version

Alyo is a mood tracker that runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We never see how you feel, what you write or when you use the app.

What stays on your device

Your check-ins — the mood score, the emotion words, the context tags, the time and any note you add — together with your reminders, language choice and settings are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Export

You can export your check-ins as a CSV file from Settings. The file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet; where it goes from there is entirely your choice.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Purchases

Alyo Premium is sold by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your name, payment details or any billing information. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Network use

The app works fully offline. Its only network activity is Apple's own App Store communication for purchases.

Deleting your data

Use “Delete all check-ins” in Settings, or simply delete the app — all locally stored data is removed with it.

Children

Because we collect no data at all, no personal information of children (or anyone else) is ever collected or processed.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
