Busy Board — Support

Walls of toys for little fingers: latches, gears, a kitchen, an orchestra, squishy things and more.

Frequently asked

How does my child play?

Everything on the wall reacts to touch: for example, flip a toggle, slide a latch, turn the gears, pull a zipper or tap the xylophone. There is nothing to win or lose and nothing to read. Pull the rope at the right edge to move to the next wall.

Where are the parent settings?

Press and hold the small lock in the top-left corner for two seconds — it grows under your finger while the ring fills — then answer the multiplication question. Children do not get in by accident.

How does the play timer work?

After a while the wall falls asleep, so play has a gentle end. In the parent settings choose how many minutes it stays awake, or ∞ for no limit. The time runs from the moment you choose it, and the line under the buttons shows how many minutes are left. Shortly before the end the wall grows dusky, then it falls asleep with a lullaby. Your choice also applies to the next play sessions.

The wall is asleep. How do I wake it?

After a rest of about half an hour it wakes up by itself the next time you open the app. To wake it sooner, open the parent settings and tap the sun button — it shows for how many minutes the wall will stay awake.

There is no sound

Check three things: the volume, Silent Mode in Control Center (the bell icon) — the app respects it — and the sound switch in the parent settings.

How do I keep my child inside the app?

Use Guided Access, a built-in iPad feature: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access. Then open Busy Board and triple-click the top button (or the Home button) to lock the iPad to the app.

Which devices and languages?

Busy Board is made for iPad; the App Store page lists the supported system versions. The app plays in landscape and needs no internet connection. There are no words in it, so it works in any language.

Are there ads or purchases?

No ads. There are no purchases in the app today; if they ever appear, they will sit in the parents' area behind the parental gate.
