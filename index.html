Flags — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

We collect no data. None.

The short version

Flags is a flag-guessing game that runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We never see who you are or how you play.

What stays on your device

Your game progress — statistics, best scores, daily-challenge history, streaks, settings and hidden flags — is stored locally on your device (and in the app's shared container so the home-screen widget can read it). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your name, payment details, or any billing information. Apple's handling of that data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders about the daily challenge are optional local notifications, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No push-notification servers are involved.

Network use

The app works fully offline. All flag images, sounds and music ship inside the app.

Deleting your data

Use “Reset progress” in the app's Settings, or simply delete the app — all locally stored data is removed with it.

Children

Because we collect no data at all, no personal information of children (or anyone else) is ever collected or processed.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds opt-in features that need it — we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
