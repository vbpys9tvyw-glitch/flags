Frequently asked

How does the streak shield work?

Each shield covers one missed day of the daily challenge — two shields can cover two days in a row. The free shield recharges after 7 completed challenges once none are left; a second one is earned by scoring 8+ in Review (once per day). Tap the flame on the daily card to see your streak calendar and shields.

What counts as “learned”?

A flag is learned after you answer it correctly at least 3 times with an overall accuracy of 70% or better. Learned flags get a badge in the Atlas and count toward the ring on the home screen.

How do I add the widget?

Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area → Edit → Add Widget → Flags. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → tap the widget area → Flags.

I don't want to see a particular flag.

Long-press any flag — in a quiz or in the Atlas — and choose “Skip this flag”. Hidden flags never appear in questions, on the home screen, or in the widget. Restore them anytime in Settings.

How do free games and Flags Pro work?

The daily challenge, Review and the Atlas are free without limits. Classic, Blitz and Mirror include 5 free games per day. Flags Pro removes the limit, adds a third shield slot and includes every future mode — as a yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → Flags Pro → “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Family Sharing is supported.
