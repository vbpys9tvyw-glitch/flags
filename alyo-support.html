Alyo — Support

A mood tracker that takes one slide: rate the moment from −10 to +10, add a few words, and see what shapes your mood.

Frequently asked

How do I log my mood?

Open the app: the Today screen is the check-in. Slide the scale, optionally tap the words that fit, and press “Save check-in”. Tags, a note and the time are under “Add details”. You can check in as many times a day as you like.

What does the scale mean?

−10 is the worst you feel, +10 the best, 0 is neutral. The words below the scale are suggestions that fit the score; “Show all emotions” opens the full list. Words are optional — a score alone is a valid check-in.

Where is my history?

Tap the calendar button on the Today screen. Every day with check-ins is a coloured dot; tap a day to see its curve through the day and all its entries. Tap an entry to edit or delete it.

What is Alyo Premium?

Insights for 30 and 90 days, energy, top emotions, weekdays and time of day, the effect of every tag, the through-the-day curve on past days, several reminders a day and CSV export. Logging, history and the 7-day insights are free forever.

I bought Premium but it says I don't have it

Settings → “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings under Subscriptions.

Can I move the scale to the other side?

Settings → Mood scale: horizontal, vertical on the left or vertical on the right. The vertical scale runs the full height of the screen — put it on the side of your thumb.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language inside the app, or iOS Settings → Alyo → Language. The app speaks 27 languages.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → “Delete all check-ins”. You get a chance to export first and a moment to undo afterwards. Deleting the app removes all data too — nothing is stored anywhere else.
